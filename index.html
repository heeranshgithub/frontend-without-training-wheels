Frequently Asked Questions

HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) is a thermoplastic polymer known for its exceptional strength-to-density ratio. It is the preferred choice for pipe systems due to its chemical resistance, flexibility, long service life of 50+ years, and cost-effectiveness compared to metal alternatives.

Our pipes are available in PN 2.5, PN 4, PN 6, PN 8, PN 10, PN 12.5, and PN 16 pressure ratings, covering a wide range of applications from low-pressure drainage to high-pressure water distribution systems.

HDPE pipes can be joined using butt fusion, electrofusion, or mechanical fittings. Butt fusion creates a joint as strong as the pipe itself, while electrofusion is ideal for confined spaces or joining pipes of different wall thicknesses.

Our pipes are certified to IS 5984, ISO 4427, and ASTM D3035 standards, and carry BIS, ISO, and CE certifications ensuring compliance with both Indian and international quality requirements.

Advanced HDPE Pipe Manufacturing Process

State-of-the-art production technology ensuring consistent quality at every step.

High-Grade Raw Material Selection

PE100 grade HDPE granules are sourced from certified suppliers and tested for melt flow index, density, and molecular weight distribution before production begins.

  • PE100 grade material
  • Optimal molecular weight distribution

Precision Extrusion Process

Advanced twin-screw extruders heat and pressurize HDPE granules to form a continuous pipe with precise wall thickness and uniform density throughout.

  • Controlled melt temperature
  • Uniform wall distribution

Controlled Cooling System

The hot extruded pipe passes through water cooling tanks that rapidly cool the pipe to lock in its shape while preventing warping or residual stress.

  • Water bath cooling tanks
  • Stress-free crystallization

Precision Sizing & Calibration

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity throughout the pipe length.

  • ±0.1mm diameter tolerance
  • Laser measurement systems

Rigorous Quality Control

Every pipe undergoes hydrostatic pressure testing, dimensional checks, and material property verification to ensure compliance with IS, ISO, and ASTM standards.

  • 100% hydrostatic testing
  • IS / ISO / ASTM compliance checks

Precision Marking & Identification

Each pipe is permanently marked with manufacturer details, pipe size, pressure rating, material grade, and production date for full batch traceability.

  • Inkjet print marking
  • Full batch traceability

Automated Cutting to Length

High-speed automatic cutters provide clean, perpendicular cuts to precise lengths as specified, ready for direct installation or coiling for transport.

  • CNC precision cutting
  • Clean perpendicular ends

Secure Packaging & Dispatch

Finished pipes are packaged in protective wrapping with end caps, bundled securely for safe transport to project sites across India.

  • End cap protection
  • Pan-India delivery network
